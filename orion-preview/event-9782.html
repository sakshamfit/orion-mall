Mother’s Day Health & Wellness Camp at Orion Mall

FROM THE ORIGINAL SITE

More from Orion

Orion Mall proudly celebrated Mother’s Day with a meaningful community initiative featuring a Free Eye Check-Up Camp and CPR Training Camp in association with Fatima Hospital.

The event witnessed enthusiastic participation from families, shoppers, healthcare professionals, and visitors who came together to promote health awareness and emergency preparedness. Guests benefited from complimentary eye screenings, expert medical guidance, and live CPR training sessions conducted by trained healthcare staff.

Along with celebrating the spirit of motherhood, the event aimed to encourage preventive healthcare and educate people about life-saving emergency response techniques. The atmosphere at Orion Mall was filled with care, learning, and community engagement, making the celebration both memorable and impactful.

Stay connected with Orion Mall for more exciting events, community activities, and celebrations.
