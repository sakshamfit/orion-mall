Republic Day Celebration at Orion Mall Gorakhpur

FROM THE ORIGINAL SITE

More from Orion

Orion Mall Gorakhpur celebrated Republic Day with great enthusiasm and patriotic spirit, creating a vibrant and festive atmosphere for shoppers and visitors.

Event Overview

The mall was beautifully themed in the colors of the national flag, reflecting pride, unity, and the true essence of Republic Day. Visitors enjoyed a lively environment filled with celebration, togetherness, and memorable moments.

Highlights of the Celebration

  • Patriotic décor across the mall premises

  • High footfall with families and shoppers participating in the celebration

  • Special shopping experiences and festive ambiance throughout the day

  • A perfect blend of celebration, shopping, and entertainment

Special Republic Day Offers

As part of the celebration, Orion Mall hosted The Big Republic Sale, offering discounts of up to 50% OFF across a wide range of brands, making it an ideal destination for festive shopping.

Celebrating the Spirit of India

The Republic Day celebration at Orion Mall Gorakhpur reaffirmed its commitment to bringing the community together through meaningful events, premium shopping experiences, and a welcoming environment for all age groups.

Orion Mall continues to be a preferred destination where celebrations, culture, and lifestyle come together under one roof.
