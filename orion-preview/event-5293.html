International Yoga Day, also known as World Yoga Day, is celebrated annually on June 21st. It was officially declared by the United Nations General Assembly in 2014, following a proposal by Prime Minister of India, Narendra Modi. The declaration aimed to raise awareness about the benefits of yoga for individuals and communities worldwide. The significance of International Yoga Day lies in promoting holistic well-being, physical fitness, and mental harmony. It is an occasion to highlight the transformative power of yoga and its potential to foster unity, peace, and sustainable development.
